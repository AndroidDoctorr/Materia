## Lashing

<img src="../../../shared/src/main/resources/assets/materia/textures/item/lashing.png" alt="Lashing" width="64" height="64">

A basic binding component used for early tools.

## Crafting

- [Recipe JSON](../../../shared/src/main/resources/data/materia/recipes/lashing.json)

Recipe:

- 3× `#forge:fibers` → 1× lashing

The recipe consumes the `#forge:fibers` tag, so any fiber item registered
to that tag works — Materia's plant fiber, or Immersive Engineering's
`hemp_fiber` when IE is installed.
