## Obtaining

Smelt the matching **Materia terracotta** block in:

- Materia **kiln** (`materia:kiln` recipes), or  
- Vanilla **furnace** / **blast furnace** (`*_smelting.json` compatibility recipes).

Recipe files: `{color}_glazed_terracotta_from_{color}_terracotta_smelting.json` under `shared/src/main/resources/data/materia/recipes/`.

## Behavior

Same as vanilla glazed terracotta: **facing** blockstate controls the directional pattern; place against the face you click.
