## Natural materials

This page covers tags for basic world / building materials that show up early in progression.

## `#materia:earth_blocks`

Used by: early stations and simple building recipes (kiln, fire pit, wattle-and-daub), and by the “clay+dirt separation” water pot recipe.

- **Tag JSON**: `shared/src/main/resources/data/materia/tags/items/earth_blocks.json`
- **Includes**:
  - `materia:earth`
  - `minecraft:packed_mud` (optional; `required: false` so older versions don’t break)

Related:

- Block: [Earth](../../content/blocks/earth.md)
- Mechanic: [Earth (and packed mud)](../../mechanics/earth-and-packed-mud.md)

## `#materia:calcites`

Used by: recipes/mechanics that accept “calcium-ish mineral inputs” (lime/calcite/bonemeal-adjacent processing).

- **Tag JSON**: `shared/src/main/resources/data/materia/tags/items/calcites.json`
- **Includes**: calcite powder, crushed shells, crushed bones
