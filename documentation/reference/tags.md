## Tags quick reference

This is a quick “what counts as what?” index for common tags referenced by recipes.

Start here for the expanded reference pages:

- [Tags (reference)](tags/README.md)
- [Mod compatibility tag bridges](tags/mod-compatibility-tags.md)

## Early crafting

- `#materia:basic_cutting_tools`: knapped flint, flint knife, bronze knife
- `#materia:basic_axes`: hand axe, wooden axe, stone axe
- `#materia:rough_planks`: all rough plank variants

See: [Early crafting and woodworking](tags/early-crafting-and-woodworking.md)

## Bindings and adhesives

- `#materia:adhesives`: pitch, resin, glue, strong glue, slime ball
- `#materia:basic_bindings`: adhesives + basic lashings
- `#materia:advanced_bindings`: strong adhesives + leather strap

See: [Bindings and adhesives](tags/bindings-and-adhesives.md)

## Metals and heat

- `#materia:heatable_metals`: items that can become hot and will burn you in your inventory

See: [Heat and hot items](tags/heat-and-hot-items.md)

## Textiles and storage

- `#materia:strings`: strings used for sewing (includes dyed strings)
- `#materia:all_needles`: needles used for sewing
- `#materia:wickers`: wicker materials for baskets

See: [Textiles and storage materials](tags/textiles-and-storage.md)

## Tools

- `#materia:all_knives`: all knife tiers
- `#materia:all_saws`: saw tiers
- `#materia:all_tongs`: tongs tiers

See: [Tools and tool-like tags](tags/tools-and-tool-like-tags.md)

## Liquids and containers

- `#materia:water`: water in cup form
- `#materia:milk`: milk containers
- `#materia:vinegar`: vinegar containers
- `#materia:lime`: quicklime/slaked lime
- `#materia:all_acids`: acid family (currently includes vinegar)

See: [Liquids and containers](tags/liquids-and-containers.md)
