## Dyes

Materia adds additional dye materials and recipes.

Start here:

- [Dyes (full section)](dyes/README.md)

## Dye tags

- `materia:green_dyes` (includes olive dye, verdigris)
- `materia:blue_dyes`
- `materia:red_dyes` (includes burgundy dye)
- `materia:yellow_dyes` (includes tan dye)
- `materia:brown_dyes` (includes tan dye)
- `materia:purple_dyes`
- `materia:lavender_dyes`

See also:

- [Verdigris](dyes/verdigris.md)
- [Burgundy](dyes/burgundy.md)
- [Tan](dyes/tan.md)
