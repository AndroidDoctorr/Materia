## Tan dye

Earth/tannin tone from brown and yellow.

## Recipe

- `minecraft:brown_dye` + `minecraft:yellow_dye` → 2× `materia:tan_dye`

## Textile products

Same pattern as olive: cotton, wool clump, wool block, string, stained glass, concrete powder, terracotta, candle.
